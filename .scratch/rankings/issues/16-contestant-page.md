# 16: Contestant page

**What to build:** Each contestant has a page showing how it's doing everywhere: its score in each category it belongs to and overall, its record, head-to-head results, and how its rating has changed over time.

**Blocked by:** 13 (Rated and unrated rankings).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The page shows the contestant's picture, name, and "Added by …" link
- [ ] It lists the contestant's score and rank in each of its categories and in overall, with rating ± range, respecting the Rated / Unrated switch
- [ ] It shows the overall win-loss record and match count
- [ ] It shows head-to-head records against its most frequent opponents
- [ ] The refresher stores weekly rating snapshots for rated global rankings, and the page draws a rating-over-time chart from them
- [ ] Contestants appear as "not yet judged" in scopes where they have no matches
